1. Setting: a, b : Fin m → Fin 3 → ℚ (or ℝ), with m, n ≥ 4, where any 3 distinct a i (resp. b j) are linearly independent (vertices of convex polygons, homogeneous coordinates).
2. P := the set of Kronecker vectors {a i ⊗ b j}. T : (Fin 3 × Fin 3 → ℚ) ≃ₗ (Fin 3 × Fin 3 → ℚ) with T '' P = P.
3. Lemma D: every 4-subset of P not inside one row {a i ⊗ b ·} or one column {a · ⊗ b j} is linearly independent. It holds for any finite dimension, given the pairwise/3-wise independence hypothesis.
4. Lemma R: ∃ σ : Perm (Fin m), τ : Perm (Fin n), with T (a i ⊗ b j) = a (σ i) ⊗ b (τ j) for all i, j; or m = n and T (a i ⊗ b j) = a (σ j) ⊗ b (τ i) (row/column incidence = rook's-graph automorphism).
5. Corollary: in that case T = gA ⊗ gB or (gA ⊗ gB) ∘ swap, with gA, gB linear and permuting the vertices. No analysis and no geometry beyond linear algebra over ℚ; the finite instances (pentagon², P5 × P7 in Q(√5, cos 2π/7)) can serve as `decide` sanity tests.
