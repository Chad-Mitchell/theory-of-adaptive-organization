**Two questions kept apart.** (Q-path) Which path between fixed ends does the minimum of a window cost select (the #309 question)? (Q-loop) What dynamics does the cost-minimizing *policy* produce in closed loop, run forward in time under noise? CORE's \(J\) is a long-run rate (`CORE.md:27`), so Q-loop is CORE's own object; #309 answered Q-path. This file answers both, arm by arm.

**Two kinds of oscillation, kept apart.** *Conservative* (Hamiltonian): energy is conserved, there is a continuum of orbits whose amplitude is set by the initial condition, no orbit attracts, and the dynamics is time-reversible; this is what real-time least action describes. *Dissipative self-sustained*: an isolated attracting cycle (limit cycle), or a noise-sustained resonance (quasi-cycle), whose amplitude and period are set by parameters, and which needs a steady throughput of free energy to persist. PAPER expectation, scored as OS-D: the oscillators Chad named (circadian clocks, heartbeats, neural rhythms, predator-prey cycles, business cycles) are of the second kind in the published record.

**What this prereg does not promise.** No derivation of mechanics, quantum theory, gravity or any force. No real system is tested. A structural minus sign in a closed-loop equation is not real-time Hamiltonian mechanics, and is not reported as such.

## 1. Classes, every price positive

All classes are linear-Gaussian unless stated, with Gaussian white noise of intensity \(\sigma^2\), additive. The heat of an actuation \(u\) is drag heat \(r\,\mathbb E[u^2]\) with \(r=\gamma>0\) (#309's class PATH, `DERIVATION.md:14` there, open loop). Stakes \(w,p\ge0\). The ruin cap is slack. Information heat on sensing is not charged unless stated (assumption S-INFO: sensing is free and noiseless; this favors the designs and is stated, not hidden).

- **Class A1 (rotating world).** World \(dZ=(-a+i\omega_0)Z\,dt+\sigma\,d\xi\) (complex, \(a>0\)), observed as \(dY=Z\,dt+\sigma_o\,d\eta\). Memory: linear, \(dM=F M\,dt+K\,dY\). Loss \(\mathbb E|Z-M|^2\).
- **Class A2 (lagged world).** World \(dx=u\,dt+\sigma\,dW\) (one integrating store). The controller sees \(x\) exactly but with lag \(\tau>0\), and knows its own past actions. Cost rate \(r\,\mathbb E[u^2]+w\,\mathbb E[x^2]\). Design: any causal policy.
- **Class B1 (reduced cost of coupled stores).** Window cost of #309's form for two stores \(x,y\): \(\int(\gamma_1\dot x^2+\gamma_2\dot y^2+w_1x^2+w_2y^2+c\,(x-y)^2)\,dt\), all coefficients \(\ge0\), and the general form: any quadratic Lagrangian whose Fourier symbol \(H(\nu)\) is Hermitian positive semidefinite at every real \(\nu\) (this includes antisymmetric, gyroscopic couplings \(g(x\dot y-y\dot x)\) when \(g^2\) is small enough).
- **Class B2 (two stores in series).** Level \(x\) fed by a reserve flow \(y\): \(\dot x=y\), \(dy=u\,dt+\sigma\,dW\). Cost rate \(r\,\mathbb E[u^2]+w\,\mathbb E[x^2]+p\,\mathbb E[y^2]\). Full state seen.
- **Class B3 (n stores in series).** \(\dot x_1=x_2,\dots,\dot x_{n-1}=x_n\), \(dx_n=u\,dt+\sigma\,dW\). Cost rate \(r\,\mathbb E[u^2]+w\,\mathbb E[x_1^2]\). Full state seen.
- **Class C1 (lagged reading, restricted design).** \(dx=-k\,x(t-\tau)\,dt+\sigma\,dW\): one store, a memoryless gain \(k>0\) on a reading delayed by \(\tau\). Cost rate \(J(k)=r\,\mathbb E[u^2]+w\,\mathbb E[x^2]=(rk^2+w)\,\mathrm{Var}_k\). Dimensionless stake \(\rho=w\tau^2/r\), \(z=k\tau\).
- **Class C2 (fixed price per action, steady drain).** \(\dot x=-d\) between resets (\(d>0\)), a reset to any level costs a fixed price \(k_a>0\), stake \(w\,x^2\). Deterministic.
- **Class C3 (delayed adaptation toward a minimum).** A design coordinate moving downhill on \(J\) with delay and a saturating step: \(\dot x=-\eta\tanh(x(t-\tau))\) (\(J''=1\), \(x^*=0\)), \(\mu=\eta\tau\).

**Effective stake, defined two ways.** *Cost-side:* integrate out the other stores at fixed \(x\) (minimize over them, frequency by frequency); the reduced symbol is \(\gamma_{\mathrm{eff}}(\nu)\nu^2+W_{\mathrm{eff}}(\nu)\). *Dynamics-side:* write the closed-loop dynamics of \(x\) alone as \(\ddot x+c_1\dot x+c_0x=\text{noise}\); then, in #309's convention \(\gamma\ddot x=Wx\) per unit inertia, \(W_{\mathrm{dyn}}=-c_0\). A real-time restoring sign means \(W_{\mathrm{dyn}}<0\).
