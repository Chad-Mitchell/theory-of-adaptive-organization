## The short answer

**Each term of J is known. The whole objective, as GTAO poses it, was not found published anywhere in the repository's searches.** The searches were not exhaustive, so "not found" is the honest word, not "new".

"All the math is known" was too blunt if it sounded like the master equation already exists elsewhere. It does not, as far as the record shows. What is known is each piece, and two-term or three-term versions of the sum.

## So what is GTAO's, in one line

The specific sum — memory price + upkeep per layer (so the number of layers is priced) + stake-weighted loss at the moment of use (lateness counted as error) + read and link costs — under a hard ruin cap, posed as the design criterion for *any* strongly selected system, with the measured world as its only input and a cross-scale kill rule. That is a new formulation and a new test (novelty 2 on the repository's scale), not a new mathematical result. Its weight, if it has any, will come from predictions that other formulations do not make, which is why the DIR1 result matters: the one prediction with opposite-sign rivals (D5) is still unscored.
