# PRIOR_ART: closest prior work for each GTAO component, and what GTAO adds

Sat 2026-11-14 ET. **Not independently reviewed.** Full citations and DOIs are in `LITMAP.md`; every DOI was resolved this pass.

**How to read the verdicts.**
- **"Adds" means: not found in this sweep.** It does not mean proven new. A sweep of this breadth cannot rule out an obscure precursor.
- Each "adds" item should get a targeted search before any public novelty claim.
- **Novelty is graded:**
  - **0:** stated before, in the same form;
  - **1:** same idea, different domain;
  - **2:** new formula or test;
  - **3:** nothing close found.

## Bottom line
- **Rules 1–7, taken one by one, are prior art.** Rule 2 is closest to Anderson & Schooler 1991 and McNamara & Houston 1987. Rule 7 and T1a are closest to Reis 2006 and Alvarez-Lippi-Paciello 2011.
- **The cross-scale "same tradeoff everywhere" claim** is anticipated:
  - in *ambition* by Friston (nested blankets) and Beer (recursive viable systems);
  - in *exact form for memory duration* by Lee-Flack-Krakauer 2024, from SFI.
- **What remains GTAO's own** (pending targeted searches):
  - the T3a threshold;
  - the T4 √N evaporation rule;
  - the temperature identity and fix-mechanism universality classes;
  - the conditions for geometric levels, and their count;
  - the Rosen + Still + lateness synthesis;
  - an explicit multi-scale, preregistered falsification program.
