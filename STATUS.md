## Where the theory stands (one paragraph)
GTAO models every adaptive organization with one budget: holding memory, prediction error (including lateness) and the reads or effort that keep memory current, plus ruin risk; only implementation and currency differ by scale. Its time-spectrum part, the map from a world's spectral slope α (with drift level q, sensing noise N and stakes) to read rate, precision effort, memory time, forgetting kernel and number of stores, is now derived, refereed and **classical**: optimal filtering and approximation theory plus a price on effort, credited (THEORY §0, §2.2, §10). Its noisy-sensing exponents (error, precision effort, memory time, kernel) hold for Gaussian worlds or the best linear tracker; the Bayes tracker of a switching world adapts on a single log and needs jump rate and amplitude separately (v1.4.3, §2.2 Regime A premise bullet; CLAIM_MAP N16). v1.4.4 (merged) states this premise in the status block, §0, §2.1 and §1 N6 too, with a lock: each prereg fixes the optimum it scores against (best linear by default). It is no longer the headline. The mode-spectrum part (§2.2 Regime C: model size ∝ Q^{1/β}, an interior minimum of the model's budget share iff β < 1, a minimum viable size from unstable modes) is PROOF under stated assumptions with known tools and was not reviewed by the spectrum-map referee. **The open, falsifiable bet is empirical** (CLAIM_MAP N13, J6): real systems at every scale sit near these optima once α, q, N and stakes are measured on the same system, with the same relations (not constants) at every level. No system has had all four inputs measured, so the bet is untested. In the model, the same three costs at every level is an axiom (Axiom 5; CLAIM_MAP J16), so the model alone cannot test it. As a claim about real systems it is testable through its consequences: at each level the three costs can be measured separately, and the claim fails if a level's design needs a fourth kind of cost to be explained, or if design does not respond to the measured prices of the three as the relations require (J6). The formal layer (sensing classes, pooling onset, vigilance, ordering, freeze/hold band) is proved in-model with corrections; an elementary result (N12, refereed and narrowed: `reviews/night2/N12_REFEREE.md`, MATH-CORRECTED): the pooling-onset exponent does not depend on α (−1 for exact reset reads, −(1+θ) for precision priced ∝ precision^θ), provided shared and private drift have the same α and reads are exact at the level; so it measures the level's technology, not the world. Vigilance growth, the mixed-technology private-drift exponent and the constants do depend on α. No field test has measured GTAO's quantities validly: nothing is confirmed or refuted in the field; field results are "passed / failed on proxy X"; vigilance is OPEN. Every prediction states its section and regime first. **Measurement:** the framework (`measurement/FRAMEWORK.md`: gates MV1–MV10, three tiers, the anti-rescue rule) re-audited every past field test and found no FIELD-PASS and no FIELD-FAIL; a failed prediction refutes theory + measurement + auxiliaries jointly, and a proxy fail kills only that mapping.
