## Where the theory stands (one paragraph)
GTAO's headline hypothesis is that the world's drift spectrum sets the exponents of an adaptive organization's design (THEORY §2): one pace → √ checking and one memory timescale (prior art); scale-free → log-spaced layers and power-law forgetting; many modes → model size ∝ Q^{1/β}. The best-proved part is the formal layer (sensing classes, pooling onset, vigilance, ordering, freeze/hold band), proved in-model with corrections; its constants are functions of the spectrum through (a, b) = 1/(1+2H) for reset reads (THEORY §2.5). The headline map is now derived and refereed (`reviews/night2/SPECTRUM_MAP/`, `reviews/night2/SPECTRUM_MAP_REFEREE.md`): it is classical filtering and approximation theory plus a price. The LFK generalization τ_m ∝ τ_E^{1−1/α} is correct in-model but holds only at fixed variance and is a published bandwidth law in substance (τ ∝ (N/q)^{1/α}; α = 2 already in Mora & Nemenman 2019). The D3 "renewal" explanation is wrong (estimator mismatch; Cox worlds give the same sign), and the locked D3S pass does not discriminate. The referee endorses and strengthens the demotion of "central new bet": the open contribution is the empirical bet that real systems sit near these optima once α, q and N are measured (wording change pending THEORY-2.2-REWRITE). No field test has measured GTAO's quantities validly, so nothing is confirmed or refuted in the field. Every prediction now states its regime first.
