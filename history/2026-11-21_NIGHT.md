## Where the bottleneck now sits

This is already stated in `OPEN_WORK.md` §8 and in `theory/PRICE_FREE_2026-11-21.md`. It is a scoped lesson, not a completed survey of field tests.

Independently sourcing fitness-unit prices (*h*, *w*) may be the binding obstacle for most field tests of the law. That is the lesson of the VIS-1F pre-Pass-1 check, together with the existing sentence in `STATUS.md` that stakes *w* have never been measured validly. The theory already says that J has free prices, so it predicts only through price-free structure or independently measured prices (`CORE.md` line 20; `THEORY.md` §4).

`PRICE_FREE` classifies what still survives when those absolute prices are unknown. As mathematics, signs, elasticities, shared-price ratios, and some thresholds remain. A few of those can still distinguish the law from named rivals, once prior art is credited. As an empirical program, no qualifying system with public, no-account data yet scores one of those short-list claims. Dropping the demand for a fitness-unit *h* and *w* therefore does not, by itself, produce a field test. It changes the bottleneck from “absolute prices” to “a short-list price-free relation, in a class that can qualify, with a second independently measured quantity (α, ρ, a sensing class, or a slack-versus-binding contrast).” That is a narrower bottleneck. It has not been cleared.

Tonight's other field-facing work sits in the same place. E. coli *N_e* is established, but σ is not, so the qualification band cannot be computed, and even with σ the locked P1/P3 predictions could not exclude rival R3. The cross-grid comparison is preregistered and cannot run on public no-account data, and would be underpowered at the locked n anyway. The fruit-fly vision candidate was dropped because the prices could not be sourced in the right units.

Until that narrower bottleneck is cleared, the law is not shown to be field-testable, with or without absolute prices.

### (a) An independent digital-evolution platform: Avida

Ofria & Wilke 2004, *Artificial Life* 10:191–229, “Avida: A Software Platform for Research in Computational Evolutionary Biology,” doi `10.1162/106454604773563612`. Crossref API verified Saturday 21 November 2026: issued 2004, authors Ofria, Wilke, type journal-article. The full published PDF was opened from CaltechAUTHORS (`https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/2h3br-gvn73`, file `OFRal04.pdf`, 205,948 bytes, SHA-256 `ae0e395b3156154d6f6aa7b57d72953b5195b6caadfe0500be28186336af9bbc`). The Michigan State author-copy URLs did not connect from this session.

The opened paper describes Avida as a software platform for experiments with self-replicating, evolving computer programs, with detailed control over experimental settings. The experimenter sets population size, mutation rates, the instruction set, and an environment file that defines resources and reactions: the computation (task) that triggers a reaction, the resource consumed, the merit effect on the organism, and any by-product. Merit is the unitless CPU speed; more resources imply a higher merit, a larger share of CPU cycles, and faster replication, which is how organisms compete for space. The default environment rewards nine boolean logic operations. Populations have a firm cap N. In short, the prices and the strength of selection are set by the experimenter, on a platform we did not write.

- Dekel & Alon 2005, *Nature* 436:588–592, “Optimality and evolutionary tuning of the expression level of a protein,” doi `10.1038/nature03842`. Crossref API verified Saturday 21 November 2026: issued 2005, authors Dekel, Alon. Official abstract opened from Europe PMC (not open access; no full-text PDF listed). The abstract says they measured the growth burden (cost) of producing and maintaining the Lac proteins, and the growth advantage (benefit) when lactose is present; that the fitness function, the difference of benefit and cost, predicts an optimal Lac expression level for each lactose environment; and that in serial-dilution evolution experiments at different lactose concentrations, cells evolved in a few hundred generations to the predicted optimal expression levels. That is a published lab case in which a cost and a stake were measured independently of the later evolved design, the environment was set by the experimenter, and evolution was compared to a predicted optimum. Their cost and benefit are of lac expression, not of GTAO's (*h*, *w*) unless a mapping is later written and locked.
- Stoebel, Dean & Dykhuizen 2008, *Genetics* 178:1653–1660, “The cost of expression of *Escherichia coli* lac operon proteins is in the process, not in the products,” doi `10.1534/genetics.107.085399`. The abstract reports a measured fitness cost of constitutive lac expression when lactose is absent, attributed to transcription and/or translation, in every environment they tested. It is a published measured cost under experimenter-controlled conditions. The abstract does not describe evolution toward a predicted optimum, so it is not, on its own, the same kind of test as Dekel & Alon.

### What such a test can and cannot establish

It can test, in a lab or in an independent digital world, whether strong selection under prices the experimenter sets finds designs near argmin J, once J is written in that world's currency and the mapping is locked before any official score. In Avida that currency is the platform's own merit, task rewards, and resources. In a Dekel-style experiment it is a measured growth cost and benefit under a set lactose environment. Those are in-model or lab-condition results. If the experimenter also varies the imposed stake or cost, comparative statics from the short list in `PRICE_FREE` can be attempted under known prices. Using a platform we did not write is a stronger check than EVO1 against the objection that we wrote the world, but it is still in silico.

It cannot establish the law in the wild. It does not show that natural systems sit near argmin J, or that natural (*h*, *w*) equal the theory's prices, or that a field class qualifies under THEORY §3 / FRAMEWORK §9. It does not replace T7, and it does not finish EVO1. Avida results remain results in a designed fitness landscape. A published evolution experiment remains a controlled world, often with strong but artificial selection. Mapping a platform's merit, or a lac expression cost, onto (*h*, *w*) and J is itself a claim that would have to be locked, not assumed.

No lock and no run are taken here.
