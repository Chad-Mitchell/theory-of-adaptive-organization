**Gate (`addendum_stability.py`).** Per property, items whose dated statement history (`regime.history`, qualifiers P580/P585/P577 since 1990) starts before 2016; per item, event counts in 2016–2020 and 2021–2025; Spearman ρ across items. STABLE iff ≥ 10 items, both halves vary, and ρ ≥ 0.3; UNSTABLE iff ρ < 0.3; otherwise NOT ASSESSABLE. Item bootstrap 95% CI (B = 500, numpy default_rng(20261115)) reported, not used in the rule. Counts of STABLE / UNSTABLE / NOT ASSESSABLE properties are reported.

**Use (sensitivity only; no verdicts).** Locked model G's β_q (same `coef_boot`, same seed and B) refit on (i) item-STABLE properties; (ii) item-STABLE ∪ negligible-drift properties (λ̂ upper bound ≤ 0.02/yr, regime moot); (iii) (lock-regime-A ∩ item-STABLE) ∪ moot. Each runs only with ≥ 5 properties and outcome variation. These cannot change P1–P4 verdicts or any claim status (the test is Tier 3 by its lock).

Declared limitation: dated qualifiers exist mostly for time-indexed properties (population, revenue, head of government); for most person and time properties the gate will be NOT ASSESSABLE, so the sensitivity may not run.
