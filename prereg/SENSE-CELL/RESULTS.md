## 2. Key numbers and sources
- Keegstra Fig 1 source data (54 cells; own computation from `FIG1.analysis`): mean Pi 0.797 (sd 0.322, median 0.847; 15% of cells > 1, 19% < 0.5), mean a0 0.303 (median 0.301). The mean Pi matches the paper's stated 0.79 +- 0.32. eps_obs = 1 - mean Pi = 0.203 [0.121, 0.290] (Lan's eps = |1 - <a>/a0| is exactly 1 - Pi).
- alpha (Lan: (kR+kB)/(2kB) = 1/(2(1-a0))) = 0.717 [0.698, 0.736]. Check: the slope of Lan's BP line in Fig 4a is ln(10^9)/28.5 kT = 0.73 by eye (not a locked input).
- Floor: Method A eps0 = 0.01 exp(5.8 alpha) = 0.64 [0.57, 0.72]; dW_BP = 1.6 kT [1.1, 2.3] per receptor per methylation time. Fuel: DG0 = 29 kT per SAM cycle (Lan 2012 text). beta = 18.
- Side check (Lan text): 10^4 receptors x 29 kT / 10 s = 2.9 x 10^4 kT/s, matching their "~3 x 10^4 kT/s (~10^3 ATP/s), 5-10% of the energy to drive a flagellar motor at 100 Hz". So Lan's cell power is the gross fuel burn; the accuracy-bearing part is dW.
- Lan 2012: eps ~ eps0 exp(-alpha dW) (Eq. 8) and Wdot ~ c0 sigma_a^2 omega_m ln(eps0/eps) (Eq. 7); both are results of a minimal stochastic model, confirmed in cells only for the speed-with-ATP and unchanged ~95% accuracy under starvation (Fig 5). Fig 4a plots eps/eps0, with eps0 the equilibrium (dW = 0) error.
- Beta at literature accuracies (post hoc, Method A, alpha 0.717): eps = 0.01 -> 5.0 (by construction), 0.05 -> 8.2, 0.10 -> 11.2, Keegstra median (0.153) -> 14.6. beta = 10 corresponds to eps = 0.080 (A) or 0.041 (F, eps0 0.33): i.e. accuracy about 92-96%.

## 3. What this says, and caveats (read before citing)
1. **H1.** Lan's relation is logarithmic in accuracy, so the local price exponent of precision in the accuracy channel is theta_eff = 1/ln(eps0/eps), 0.24-0.39 (A) and up to 0.53 (F) for 95-99% accuracy, not the default theta = 1. By N12 the formal onset exponent for such a channel is between -1 (per-nat) and -(1+theta_eff) = about -1.24 to -1.4 (not -2); the tracking-layer elasticities with the world slope alpha_w = 2 would be a = 2 theta/(2 theta+1) = 0.32-0.45 and b = theta/(2 theta+1) = 0.16-0.23 rather than 2/3 and 1/3 (derived here, **not scored, unreviewed**). The status is INCONCLUSIVE because the 0.5 edge is not cleared under Method F. It does show that the θ = 1 default of THEORY §2.2's precision row is a choice that the cell's accuracy channel does not make; the receptor-read channel (Berg-Purcell, cost roughly linear in receptor number) is a second channel with theta near 1 that was not scored (L3).
2. **H2 is the substantive result: the FRAMEWORK §3.11 sentence "beta_phys ~ 1-10 at the cell" failed on this proxy.** The cell burns 29 kT per cycle and the measured whole-array accuracy (80%) needs only about 0.7-2 kT on Lan's best-performance line, so the margin over the floor is about 15-40x, not <= 10x. This is not a field kill.
3. **Caveats that could move H2 (L7, not in the prereg, found on reading Keegstra):** (a) Lan's anchor (~99%, ΔW = 5.8 kT) is for the Tar receptor, whereas Keegstra's Pi is for the whole mixed Tar/Tsr array under a saturating MeAsp step; Keegstra states the mean 0.79 "agrees well with population measurements (Neumann et al. 2014)" and attributes the spread to variation in the Tar/Tsr ratio, and Lan notes Tsr is less accurate. At the literature accuracies for Tar (95-99%), beta = 5-8, inside the band (post hoc, Method A). (b) Lan's 20% efficiency is an assumption, so Method A fixes beta = 5 at 99% by construction. (c) Pi is noisy single-cell FRET (15% of cells above 1). (d) The floor is a model line, not a measured one. The honest reading: **beta lies in the 5-40 range, about the edge of the band, and which side depends on whether the array-level or the Tar-level accuracy is the relevant output**; the prereg's primary proxy gives FAIL.
4. **H4 is untestable now**, so the cross-scale claim that the *observed class equals the class the floor selects* has no cell test yet. What would settle it: effort (adaptation rate or kinase noise) of cells measured under ligand ensembles of controlled fluctuation level and benefit, with energy state recorded. I did not find such a public dataset.

## 4. Plain-English read for the cross-scale bet (3 sentences)
At cell scale the physics does give the theory a definite price structure (cost grows with the log of accuracy, so θ is well below 1 for the accuracy channel), but the published cell data say real cells spend roughly 15-40 times the minimum free energy needed for the accuracy they show, so the theory's "physics binds tightly at the cell" is only borderline supported, and it fails the band it set for itself on the locked proxy. It still sits orders of magnitude closer to the floor than neurons (~10^5) or firms, so the ordering "physics matters more at small scale" survives, but that is a weak, non-discriminating check. Nothing here tests whether cells tune their sensing as the tracking layer says, so the strongest-selection cross-scale bet (N6/N13) is unchanged.
