## 0. Why this target, and the candidates that were scored

The prediction under test is the one in `theory/GENERAL_FORM.md` §7 (Q5), claim-map row **GF5 (OPEN)**. A fitted antitone cloud is not that prediction. Excess over the joint information relaxation is not a miss of the achievable front. A miss needs a dominating score that is achievable, or excess over a bound that is proved tight for the preregistered class. The read-side sequential rate–distortion bound of a scalar Gauss–Markov world is tight: the Kalman filter attains it (`theory/SHANNON_2026-11-21.md` §3). The joint memory-and-read corner is not tight on the published grid (efficiency 0.9574), so this lock does not score that corner.

## 2. Hypothesis this lock scores

On a block whose stimulus passes the class gate, a design that qualifies under section 3 has directed-information read rate within the locked band of the floor computed from that stimulus's own (a, Q) at the design's own posterior variance P.

A sample that is antitone, or undominated, in (r, D) does not meet the hypothesis. The shifted scenario in the simulator is antitone and is above the floor; its label is fails to support.

## 6. Computed front

For each block that passes the class gate, the front's read coordinate at the achieved P is r_floor above. It is computed from the stimulus half that the decoder does not score. No price is fit. No isotonic curve is substituted for r_floor. The memory coordinate of the relaxation is computed and stored beside the block and is not compared with a threshold.

The rigid shift of +0.40 nats in r at fixed P, the shift GF5 uses as arithmetic, is a point a Kalman filter dominates, because the read bound is attained. That shift is the simulated rival. It is not a description of the deposit.

## 7. Test statistic and thresholds

Let the excesses be the blocks that passed the class gate. Let m be their mean and s their standard error (population standard deviation divided by sqrt(n)). Let half = 1.96 s. Constants: τ = 0.15 nats, bias bound = 0.05 nats, N_MIN = 8, Z = 1.96.

The rules are applied in order. The first one that fires is the label.

| Order | Condition | Label |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Qualification is not `established` | **not a test** (qualification) |
| 2 | The class gate fails for the task (including schema failure, or every natural-movie block dropped) | **not a test** (world class or schema) |
| 3 | Fewer than 8 finite excesses | **not a test** (too few blocks) |
| 4 | half is not finite, or half ≥ τ | **not a test** (resolution). The support band has to be wider than the interval half-width. |
| 5 | m + half < −0.05 | **not a test** (below the floor). A tight bound cannot be beaten; this is a measurement or class failure. |
| 6 | m + half ≤ 0.15 and m − half ≥ −0.05 | **supports** |
| 7 | m − half > 0.15 | **fails to support** |
| 8 | otherwise | **not a test** (unresolved) |

**Supports** means the read rate is within the band of the world-computed floor, under a qualification record that this lock does not yet have. It does not mean the cloud was antitone.

**Fails to support** means the read rate is above that floor by more than the band, so a Kalman reader at the same P dominates, on a class that qualifies. It is a miss of this preregistered comparison. It is not, by itself, a class-level conclusion under the coverage rule until n_min and c are in force, which they are only after qualification is established. With the allowance in section 3, one such miss on the one design meets the allowance.

**Not a test** means the comparison was not available. It is not support and it is not a miss.

The official real-data row of this lock fires rule 1.

### Cloud report, not a threshold

`dominated_fraction` is the share of points strictly dominated in (r, D). `cloud_isotonic_mse` is the mean squared residual about a decreasing fit. A later reader can see a cloud that is undominated while the label is fails to support. That pattern is the GF5 separation. It is not a second way to support the hypothesis.

## 8. Power and sensitivity

FRAMEWORK MV10 allows a simulation of the locked decision code. `power_study` draws on-floor Kalman blocks and blocks whose plug-in rate is shifted by +0.40 nats, at the locked T_MIN, N_MIN, and seed family. The gate is met when the frequency of **supports** given the on-floor world is at least 0.8 and the frequency of **fails to support** given the shifted world is at least 0.8. Both labels are reachable on their own best-case draws; the simulator's fixed scenarios are those draws (on-floor; shifted; below-floor; non-Gauss–Markov; qualification absent; memoryless observation).

The known-groups check (MV7) in this lock is that simulation: the Kalman design is the group that sits on the bound, and the shifted design is the group that does not. No real series is the known group. MV7 is therefore not met for a field claim. MV9's external timestamp still needs Chad's go. MV5's second estimator is in the class gate and is exercised on the simulated series. **Tier of this lock: Tier 3.** A field comparison after an addendum stays Tier 3 until the gates a later preregistration claims are actually met on the real blocks.

Sensitivity that is already in the rules, not a search: if the class gate drops the natural-movie blocks, the label is not a test rather than a miss; if the plug-in rate falls below the floor by more than the bias bound, the label is not a test rather than support; if the interval half-width reaches τ, the label is not a test. Those three are the ways a misspecified world or a biased estimator is kept out of the two decisive labels.

## 10. If a later qualified run fails to support: diagnosis, not a patch

This file is not edited after `LOCK.txt`. A change is a dated `DEVIATIONS.md` that does not alter the locked hash, or it is a new preregistration. The order of a diagnosis, each item its own later preregistration, is:

1. **Measurement.** Plug-in bias, train/test leakage, bin edges, unit-selection, and whether the second-half P and the first-half (a, Q) were actually kept apart. The below-floor rule is the symptom of an estimator that understates rate.
2. **Inputs.** The movie is a laboratory world. A class gate can pass on a series the lineage was not selected in. Re-measuring the world is allowed once, before a comparison, in a new preregistration, not after a miss inside this one.
3. **Hidden cost.** A cost of spiking, or of an error this squared loss does not represent, would mean the lineage is minimizing a different coordinate. That is a candidate term, recorded as such. It is not added to this label. GB6 already says the term list is an input.
4. **Claim.** The tight bound may be the wrong bound: non-quadratic loss, lead not zero, or a source the Gaussian plug-in does not describe. The fix is a new preregistration with a different locked class. It is not a rereading of these thresholds.

A not-a-test from qualification, from the class gate, or from a rate below the floor is not a miss and is not support. The anti-rescue clause still applies to any later claim that a completed comparison "missed how the system really works" unless that claim names a gate the comparison failed.
