**Subject:** Same-cell FRET noise and adaptation time across CheR/CheB levels?

Dear Dr. Emonet [or Dr. Cluzel / Dr. Park],

I am testing a simple theory of optimal memory in sensing systems, using E. coli chemotaxis as the main case.
Your single-cell work (Park et al. 2010, Nature, doi 10.1038/nature09551; Keegstra et al. 2017, eLife,
doi 10.7554/eLife.27455) is the closest data to what the test needs, and I have used the published figures
(Fig. 4c of Park et al. 2010; the open per-cell data of Keegstra et al. 2017) with full credit.

The test needs, in the **same cells**, both (i) the spectrum or variance of the steady-state FRET (kinase
activity) fluctuations and (ii) the step-response adaptation time, across several CheR/CheB induction levels at
fixed adapted activity a0. From those I estimate how the store's own noise scales with its time constant (an
exponent s); the published data give s ≈ 0.3 with a wide interval (Keegstra: −0.28 to 0.88).

Do you have unpublished single-cell data of that kind, or would you consider running the measurement? I would
share the full preregistered analysis plan (fixed before seeing any data), credit the data as you wish, and send
you the result whatever it shows.

Thank you for considering it.
