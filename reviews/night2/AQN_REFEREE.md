## 0. Verdict
**PASS-WITH-FIXES for the estimators. E-A2 FAILS as written. E-A1 and E-A3 pass with fixes. E-A4 and E-A5 pass with a wording fix.** The Whittle core (spectral model, aliasing, normalisation, Wald SEs, the demo numbers) is confirmed with independent code. Five corrections:
1. **The sample-length rule SE(α̂) ≈ 1/√M understates the SE by 1.5–2.3× when the noise floor is above Nyquist (ω_c ≳ π)**, which is the common case for clean data. DGS10 itself shows this: SE 0.026 against 1/√M = 0.015 (§V3).
2. **The premise test is blind to jump-diffusion worlds.** It rejected 0 of 24 jump-diffusion random walks whose jumps carry half the variance (excess kurtosis 52), and the kurtosis screen flagged 24 of 24. So E-A2, which replaces the kurtosis screen with that test, would remove the only screen that sees compound-Poisson jumps (§V7).
3. **R1 does more than leave a gap: it mislabels a random walk as Regime B.** THEORY §2.2 defines A as α = 2 and gives "a random walk with white increments" as its first example, and defines B as α ≠ 2. A corrected rule R1′ is given in §V6.
4. **"Validated t_A" means the plug-in formula is estimated without bias, not that the formula is right on the grid.** In block C's cells the plug-in t_A is 0.6–14 steps, outside the continuous-time domain. There my own exact discrete-time filter takes 1.14–1.89× longer to cross over (§V8).
5. **The replicate-measurement IV stakes estimator is unbiased only under additive error that is independent of e.** Under multiplicative error it is biased by 1/(1 + c²) (§V10).
