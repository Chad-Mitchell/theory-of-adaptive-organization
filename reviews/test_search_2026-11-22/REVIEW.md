3. **D2's count is the repository's from-0 count. The implementation and the Kelly coincidence use different penalties.** `theory/TEST_SEARCH_2026-11-22.md:45` and `:109–113`. The formula and \(R=w E_\infty/h\) match the layer-count rule when decrements are non-increasing. A large \(h\) leaves \(K^\star_0\) below the number of positive decrements. That part is right, and it is Fox's marginal count, already marked as such in L1.

   The coincidence is right for a deterministic cost: if replication is any strictly decreasing function of \(hK+w\mathbb{E}[\ell]\), including a linear fall and the map \(W=\exp(-J)\), Kelly and the cost pick the same \(K^\star\). The implementation in `:109` charges \(h\) CPU cycles per extra register and \(w\) merit per unit tracking error. Per-unit tracking error is the realized residual. A linear penalty on that residual does not force the rankings together. Hand example, both designs scored with \(W=6-hK-\ell\) and \(w=1\):

   | Design | Law of \(\ell\) | \(J=hK+\mathbb{E}[\ell]\) | \(\mathbb{E}[W]\) | \(\mathbb{E}[\log W]\) |
   |---|---|---:|---:|---:|
   | A | \(\ell\in\{0,3\}\) with equal probability | 1.5 | 4.5 | 1.445185878948082 |
   | B | \(hK=0.4\), \(\ell=1.2\) certain | 1.6 | 4.4 | 1.481604540924216 |

   The cost and \(\mathbb{E}[W]\) pick A. \(\mathbb{E}[\log W]\) picks B. So ":113", "Kelly, if replication falls linearly with \(hK+wE\), picks the same count," is true when \(E\) is the expected error inside a deterministic offspring map. It is a different sentence from "charge \(w\) merit per unit tracking error."

   Under the deterministic reading the note uses for the coincidence, Kelly is the closer rival whenever the cycle cost sits inside replication, and (d) against Kelly is no. The note says that, and then marks (d) yes against mode-matching. Criterion (d) asks for the closest rival. Mode-matching keeps one store per environmental state at every \(h\). It is the farther call. D2's own last paragraph (`:117`) says D2 does not answer Kelly. The (d) yes on `:113` answers a rival the same sentence calls farther.

   Under the realized-error reading, Kelly can disagree with the cost, and the cell "(d) is no" against Kelly is not established. Either reading, the protocol does not currently state a single prediction that meets (d) against the rival it calls closer.
