## 2. Proved versus conjectured (my reading; I am not refereeing an 83-page paper in 20 minutes)
- **What is a derivation inside the framework:** items 1–3, 6, 7, 10, 11 are calculations once the framework (first-order GR, null-cone data, Gaussian representation, gluing = diffeomorphism invariance) is accepted.
- **The headline, finite dim H_D (item 5), is CLAIMED with a proof sketch whose weak points are visible:**
  - it is a proof by contradiction that leans on gluing reproducing the uncut amplitude (the paper's diffeomorphism-invariance axiom, §3.6/(4.29)), i.e. on the framework, not on independent physics;
  - §5.1's state-existence step contains an admitted heuristic (fn 18);
  - representation dependence is open (fn 20);
  - "maximum size" is defined by a bound on a **gravitational clock** (affine-parameter reading at the corner), and finiteness comes from **4D focusing/shear** (gravitons raise the clock reading at fixed area), which is absent in JT (item 4).
- **What it does not give:** a general inequality log dim H_D ≤ A/4G_Nħ for an arbitrary diamond. The covariant entropy bound enters only as motivation: "semiclassically the covariant entropy bound [66] suggests that the corner area limits the number of orthogonal states" (:2244). A quantitative count appears only in the large-black-hole limit (item 8). So "how many states fit in a finite region" is answered as "finitely many" (CLAIMED), with "≈ e^{A/4G_Nħ}" only asymptotically for marginally trapped corners.
- **Relation to Bousso:** Bousso 1999, "A covariant entropy conjecture" (10.1088/1126-6708/1999/07/004, KNOWN) is cited once, as semiclassical motivation. The paper does not prove the Bousso bound.
- **Relation to Jacobson 2016** ("Entanglement equilibrium and the Einstein equation", 10.1103/PhysRevLett.116.201101, KNOWN): **not cited** (the only Jacobson reference is Andrade e Silva–Jacobson on 2+1 causal diamonds). The logic runs the other way: Jacobson 2016 assumes maximal vacuum entanglement in small balls, the first-order relation δS = δ⟨K⟩ and small-ball BW, and outputs the Einstein equation; Chandrasekaran **assumes** GR (its action and G_N) and quantizes it, then recovers semiclassical Einstein equations as the low-energy limit. The area-entropy coefficient 1/(4G_Nħ) is fixed by the boost charge A/8πG_N, so "universal η" is tied to G here as in Jacobson 1995, not explained.
- **Relation to Chandrasekaran–Longo–Penington–Witten** ("An algebra of observables for de Sitter space", JHEP02(2023)082, 10.1007/JHEP02(2023)082, KNOWN) and **CPW** ("Large N algebras and generalized entropy", 10.1007/JHEP04(2023)009, KNOWN): cited for the statement that perturbative bulk algebras of black-hole exteriors and static de Sitter patches contain an observer/boundary Hamiltonian, and for the Bekenstein–Hawking term at finite G_N [18–20]. **Type II algebras are never mentioned in the text** (no "type II" anywhere in paper.txt). In CLPW/CPW and Witten's crossed product (10.1007/JHEP10(2022)008, KNOWN) the perturbative (G → 0) algebras are type II₁ (de Sitter) and type II∞ (black hole), which give entropy differences but not a state count. This paper reports a **type I** factor with finite dimension at finite G_N. My reading (HYP, not stated by the paper): this is the nonperturbative completion one would hope the type II algebras approximate, with finite dimension replacing the renormalized trace. Whether the two match in the G → 0 limit is not shown.
- **Independent check status:** none yet. The paper is a day old. Treat everything above as CLAIMED until there is refereeing or expert follow-up.
