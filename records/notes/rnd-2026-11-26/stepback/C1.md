*26 Nov 2026. Local note only (not committed or pushed). Self-checked only; it needs a hostile review before it counts. Labels: **PIM**, **KNOWN**, **ELEMENTARY**, **HYP**, **OBSERVED** (numerical output of `c1/*.py`), **CHOSEN** (an input put in by hand).*

## 1. Statement and criteria (written 09:56 EST, before any C1 code was run; exploratory, not a hash-locked prereg)

**Route C1, from STEPBACK §5.** In 2D lattice vacua, is the cost boundary sharp and isotropic, with per-layer marginal information per boundary site within 20% of κ, in critical and gapped vacua and for 1–3 species? STEPBACK's own note on the row: for independent species, "marginal = κ" holds by the first-order condition, so the species part is not a test. Only shape and sharpness are tests.

**Setup (scheme A of G4-1, carried to 2D; every choice named).**
- **World (declared inputs, not tested).** The ground state of a free-fermion square-lattice vacuum at half filling, infinite plane, with correlations from the k-space projector. Four vacua:
  - (V1) nearest-neighbour hopping, critical, with a square Fermi surface;
  - (V2) V1 plus a staggered mass m(−1)^{x+y}, m = 0.5, gapped;
  - (V3) π-flux hopping, critical, with two Dirac cones (Lorentz-like at low energy);
  - (V4) V3 plus a staggered mass m = 0.5, a gapped Dirac vacuum.
- **Tracker.** The stake region T is the 2×2 plaquette at the origin. The value of a record S is I(T : S) in bits.
- **Price.** κ per stored site (CHOSEN: per-site pricing is a locality choice, as in G4-1 scheme A).
- **Rule.** Greedy: repeatedly add the candidate site with the largest gain in I(T : S); stop when the best gain is < κ. The greedy order does not depend on κ, so one run gives every κ.
  - Candidates are unselected sites within Chebyshev distance 3 of T ∪ S, inside a disk of radius 30 (CHOSEN, for speed; checked against a wider window on one vacuum).
  - Each run is capped at 200 added sites.
- **Species.** n identical independent copies on each site, stored together, so a site's gain is n times the one-copy gain.

**Criteria (fixed now).** Evaluated at the κ where |S| first reaches at least 120 sites, call it κ_min, and at the run's last κ if |S| never reaches 120.
- **Sharp (H1-2D):** T ∪ S fills ≥ 80% of the lattice sites inside its convex hull, and r99 − r90 ≤ 3 (radial weight about the plaquette centre, as in G4-1).
- **Isotropic (ISO):** boundary sites are selected sites with an unselected nearest neighbour. Bin their radii into 8 angular sectors and take the mean radius r(θ) per sector. Pass iff (max − min)/mean ≤ 0.20.
- **Shadow price (SP):** for the inward layer L_in (the boundary sites of S) and the outward layer L_out (unselected sites adjacent to S ∪ T), compute
  - ρ_in = [I(T:S) − I(T:S∖L_in)]/(κ·|L_in|) and
  - ρ_out = [I(T:S∪L_out) − I(T:S)]/(κ·|L_out|).
  - Pass iff both lie in [0.8, 1.2].
- **Expectations, stated for honesty and not as tests:**
  - V1 fails ISO, because the square Fermi surface makes correlations anisotropic and half filling zeroes C(x, y) at even x + y.
  - V3 and V4 are closer to isotropic.
  - SP is near FOC-trivial for ρ_out, which is < 1 at the stopping point for the next site. Layer averages can still miss the 20% band.

- (c) The r99−r90 test is weak here. The radial weight (greedy gains) is dominated by the eight sites nearest T, so r90 ≈ 2.5–4.3 throughout.
- (d) One tracker shape (2×2) and one mass value were used.

## 3. Core step (paper)

**Lemma C1-FOC (ELEMENTARY, holds for any value function).** For the greedy rule at price κ, the last site added has gain ≥ κ, and the best next site has gain < κ. So the single-site marginal at the cut brackets κ by construction. This is the whole of "marginal = κ" that follows from the first-order condition. It says nothing about layers: ρ_in and ρ_out average joint gains over a whole layer, and I(T:·) is neither additive nor submodular across a layer. The numbers show both directions of non-additivity:
- removing the inner layer together costs 1.3–3.3 κ per site (complementarity among boundary sites);
- adding the outer layer together gives only 0.4–0.7 κ per site (redundancy).

**Result (OBSERVED, 4/4 vacua):** the C1 claim "per-layer marginal per boundary site within 20% of κ" is an **open miss** in critical and gapped vacua alike. The shadow-price reading holds only at the single-site margin, where it is ELEMENTARY.

**Lemma C1-SP (ELEMENTARY).** Take n identical independent species stored together per site. Then I(T⊗n : S⊗n) = n·I(T:S), so the greedy order is unchanged and the record at (κ, n) equals the record at (κ/n, 1). Species never change the per-site marginal at the cut; they move the cut outward, as if the price were renormalised to κ/n. This is checked numerically above (a code identity, not a test). *Analogy only, not a derivation:* Susskind–Uglum 1994 absorb the species dependence of the entanglement entropy into the renormalisation of Newton's constant. Here the species dependence is absorbed into κ → κ/n. Nothing about gravity follows.

**Isotropy (HYP, supported by 2 vs 2 vacua).** The price is isotropic by construction (κ per site), so any anisotropy of the cut comes from the vacuum's correlations.
- Observed: the Dirac vacua (π-flux, Lorentz-like low-energy dispersion) give isotropic, sharp cuts whether critical or gapped. The NN vacua (square Fermi surface; the gapped V2 inherits the same lattice structure) give cuts elongated along the diagonals by ≈ 1.6× whether critical or gapped.
- So, in this sample, shape tracks the vacuum family, not criticality. That differs from the 1D-style expectation "gapped sharp, critical ragged" (G4-1 errata E-G3G5-5), though the geometries differ.
- Reading: the isotropy of the record boundary is an input from the vacuum's low-energy symmetry (Lorentz-like or not), not an output of the cost. This is what the STEPBACK row said a failure of isotropy would name.

**Jacobson 2016 bridge (status).** Jacobson's entanglement equilibrium is a first-order stationarity condition (δS = 0 at fixed volume, for small balls). Lemma C1-FOC is the discrete counterpart for this cost: stationarity at the margin. The layer-level open miss means that stationarity does not extend to a "per-site boundary price" in these lattice vacua. The bridge stays a single-site analogy (HYP that it is more), with no area-law or Einstein-equation content claimed.
