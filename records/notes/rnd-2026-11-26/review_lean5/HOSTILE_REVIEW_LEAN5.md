## 2. M11Blocks: do the statements say what the prose claims?

**Definitions (faithful).** A block part is `{a : Σ_{i∈block k} |a_i| ≤ 1 ∀k}`. Its facets are `1 + s·a` with
`s` = ±1 on one block, 0 elsewhere (`IsBlockSign`), and its vertices have one ±1 per block (`IsBlockVert`).
`maxSetB` is `x₀₀ = 1` plus every facet⊗facet product ≥ 0. For a polytope part with all effects, the effect cone
is generated by the facets, so this is the maximal tensor product in local-tomographic coordinates. `pureSetB` is
the set of products of vertices. Instances: `maxSet_eq_blocks` (id, id), `maxSetX_eq_blocks` (one block each) and
`maxSetCX_eq_blocks` (id, one block) recover round 4's sets and the mixed cube × cross-polytope sets exactly.

**`m11_blocks`.** For a finite group `Γ` and `ρ : Γ →* Matrix` whose image contains (hsep) for each `u ≠ v` a
diagonal `diag d` with `d u d v ≠ 1`, and (htrA/htrB) for each same-block pair a lifted coordinate permutation
sending one coordinate to the other: every `ρ γ0` with `ρ γ0 '' S = S` maps `pureSetB` onto itself, for any
`pureSetB ⊆ S ⊆ maxSetB`. The proof chain is real:
- `avgGram` is invariant, so `diag_of_separating` makes it diagonal.
- `perm_conj_entry` makes it constant on blocks.
- `quad_blockw` writes it as a weighted sum of block norms.
- `block_le` (from `maxSetB_blockA/B/C`, which come from the facet inequalities) bounds each norm by its value at
  a product state.
- `weighted_le` and `pure_of_eqB` give equality only at products.
- `M11Prime.final_assembly` closes with `E := S`.

**`m11_blocks_polytope`.** S = conv V, V finite. It builds `ρ : autSet S →* Matrix` from `Units.coeHom ∘
subtype`, with `Fintype` from `autSet_finite V hV hspan`. The diagonal maps and permutations named in the
hypotheses enter as elements of `autSet S` (`diagUnit`, `permUnit`), and `T` itself enters as `⟨T, hT⟩`.
**Aut(S) is built, not assumed.** The only symmetry inputs are invariance hypotheses on S, as disclosed.

**Generality the prose understates (harmless, L3).**
- `hsep` accepts any ±1 diagonal map of S, not only local ones.
- `htrA` accepts any permutation of A's coordinates that sends `i` to `i'`, not only within-block ones.
Both make the Lean statement stronger than the prose.

**Coverage against §17 M11′.** Covered: both parts products of cross-polytopes (cube, cross-polytope, mixed,
octahedron × octahedron, and products with unequal blocks); S a polytope between min and max; S invariant under
separating diagonal symmetries and within-block transitivity. Not covered, and disclosed: free sums (cube ⊕ cube),
other 2-level parts, non-polyhedral S.

**Is the subgroup-invariance claim proved?** The README row for `diag_of_separating` (labelled **Faithful**)
says: "Among diagonal symmetries the condition is also necessary for this step: if no `d` in the family
separates `u ≠ v`, the matrix `E_uv + E_vu` is invariant under all of them." **No Lean declaration states this.**
It is asserted only. The statement is true. I proved it in a scratch file (`scratch/Necessity.lean`, compiles,
standard axioms): for `u ≠ v` and `d u * d v = 1`, `(diag d)ᵀ (E_uv + E_vu) diag d = E_uv + E_vu`. It is
necessity for the Schur step only. It is not necessity for M11′'s conclusion, and the README's wording ("for
this step") is correct on that point (M1).

**Are the hypotheses satisfiable?** `blocks_hyps_consistent` proves only `V finite ∧ conv V ⊆ maxSetB ∧ pureSetB
⊆ conv V` for `V = pureSetB`. It leaves out hsep/htr/hfA/hfB, and the README says so in its own row. As
supporting evidence, I proved in `scratch/NonVac.lean` (compiles) that `conv(pureSetB)` is invariant under every
A-flip `diag(rowSign i)`; hfB is symmetric. Within-block transpositions permute block vertices on paper, so the
theorems are not vacuous. The PR draft's "non-vacuity: `blocks_hyps_consistent`" overstates this (M2).
